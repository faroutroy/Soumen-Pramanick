Publications

An Ethereum based Fake Product Identification System using Smart Contract

IEEE Conference Publication
Published: June 8, 2022

In recent years, counterfeit products have played an important role in the product making industry, affecting companies' brands, sales, and profits. This research presents a blockchain-based solution to identify authentic products and detect counterfeits. The system leverages blockchain's distributed, immutable digital ledger to store business data as blocks in multiple repositories linked by chains. The technology ensures that no block can be changed or modified, providing robust security. The proposed system uses unique serial numbers assigned to products, eliminating the need for intermediaries to ensure product security. By generating distinct product codes and matching them against the Blockchain network, customers can verify product authenticity, receiving notifications about whether a product is genuine or counterfeit.
